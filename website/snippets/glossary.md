*[NPX]: Normalized Protein eXpression: Olink's value per protein, on a log2 scale (+1 NPX means twice as much)
*[LOD]: Limit of detection: below it, the signal cannot be told apart from background noise
*[MissingFreq]: Missing frequency: Olink's column with the share of samples below the LOD for each assay (0.12 = 12%)
*[PEA]: Proximity extension assay: the technology Olink uses to measure proteins
*[bridge samples]: The same samples measured on two plates, on purpose, so the plates can be lined up
*[bridge sample]: The same sample measured on two plates, on purpose, so the plates can be lined up
*[reference plate]: The plate whose values stay as they are; the other plates are put on its scale
